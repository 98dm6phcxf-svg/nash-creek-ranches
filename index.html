The Development

Nash Creek Land Plan

Nash Creek Ranches is situated just off Old Seguin Luling Road in Guadalupe County, Texas — minutes from Seguin with quick access to HWY 90 and I-10. The development features 12 thoughtfully laid-out tracts accessed via a 60-foot road easement and private caliche road. Lot sizes range from 10 to 16± acres, each with mature oaks, dense hardwoods, and gently rolling terrain. A wet-weather creek meanders throughout, supporting abundant native wildlife.

Why Nash Creek

Land Built for Texas Living

📍

Prime Location

Off Old Seguin Luling Road in Guadalupe County with quick access to HWY 90 and I-10. Seguin is minutes away; San Antonio ~50 minutes; Austin ~1 hour; Houston under 2 hours.

🌳

Natural Beauty

Dense hardwoods, scattered mature oaks, and gently rolling terrain. A wet-weather creek meanders through portions of the ranch, supporting whitetail deer, turkey, hogs, and dove.

⚡

Utilities & Infrastructure

Electricity available at the road. Surrounding water wells tap the Carrizo Aquifer — one of Texas's most reliable water sources. Private caliche road with 60-foot road easement.

💼

Investment Potential

Agricultural valuation keeps property taxes low. Guadalupe County is one of the fastest-growing regions in Central Texas. Flexible terms: cash, conventional, owner financing, Texas Vet.
